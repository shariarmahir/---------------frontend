# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Bangladeshi citizens, families and village communities who live with failing public services (healthcare first), many on mid- or low-end Android phones over patchy 3G/4G. Alongside them: partners, researchers, interns and investors deciding whether Kandari-Lab is serious enough to back or join.

## Product Purpose

The company website for Kandari-Lab (কাণ্ডারী-ল্যাব), a Bangladeshi deep-tech startup (semiconductors, IoT, robotics, AI/ML, hardware + software). It tells the story (problem, challenge, vision, mission, goal, impact), shows the products and services, and converts visitors into Kandari Profile members and app users. Success: a visitor understands what the company fixes and why, and joins.

## Positioning

Bangladesh is treated as a corrupted, low-resolution image; every failing public service is one bad pixel. Kandari-Lab repairs the map one sector at a time with its own hardware, software and AI, built in Bangladesh by people who live with the problems. Healthcare is the first sector: care within the "Golden Two Hours", in every village ("One Village, One Medical Health Care Center", starting with Kazaikat, Nakla, Sherpur).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products: SWASTI (স্বস্তি), a Bangla voice-first health super-app (RAG medical agent, CNN diagnostics), in beta; আপনজন-Aponjon, a wearable vitals/neuro device, prototype; Smart Pharmacy, pilot. চেতনা, a members-only skills-and-work platform (/media).
- Services: AI, hardware/IoT, signal and software builds for factories, offices, farms and apps.
- R&D (in research, not shipping): blind-assistance wearable, organic-waste-to-soil, semiconductor/data work.
- Membership: Kandari Profile (phone OTP first; mock gateway, no backend yet).
- Open decisions (CLAUDE.md §9): backend, CMS, SMS provider, colour hierarchy sign-off, hero ratio on mobile, "Motto" spelling.

## Brand Commitments

- Name Kandari-Lab (কাণ্ডারী-ল্যাব); Bangla-first names and motto, set in a real Bangla face (Hind Siliguri), never a fallback.

- Founder: Mahir Shariar Mahin (CEO). Team per CLAUDE.md §2.

## Product Principles

1. Built here, for here: problems solved by the people who live with them.
2. Open to all: priced and designed for every village, not only the cities.
3. Proof before promises: show what works; call research what it still is.
4. One pixel at a time: one sector done properly before the next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere. Pages must stay light on low-end phones and slow networks (lazy-load below the fold, no heavy WebGL by default). Bangla text needs its own line-height.
